# `util.calculate-circle-center-3pt()` — Circumcentre Of 3 Points

Returns the centre of the unique circle that passes through three
points. Used internally by `circle-through` and `arc-through`.

## Signature

```text
calculate-circle-center-3pt(a: vector, b: vector, c: vector) -> vector
```

## Parameters

- **`a`**, **`b`**, **`c`** — the three points.
