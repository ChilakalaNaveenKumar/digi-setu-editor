# `vector.cross()` — Cross Product

Returns the 3D cross product of two vectors.

## Signature

```text
cross(v1: vector, v2: vector) -> vector
```

## Parameters

- **`v1`**, **`v2`** — operands. The result is a vector perpendicular to
  both, with magnitude equal to the area of the parallelogram they span.
