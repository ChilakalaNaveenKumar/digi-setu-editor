| [`01-fit-to.md`](./01-fit-to.md) | `utils.fit-to-height` and `utils.fit-to-width` for scaling content to a target dimension. |

These are convenience helpers built on Typst's `measure()` + `scale()`.
Use them to fit titles, tables, or images to the available slide
height / width without computing the exact scale factor by hand.
